## Frictional log (10% of this assignment)

Keep an honest log of how you did this work, and submit it with the work. It is not a timesheet and not a performance of suffering. Write short dated entries while you work, one per work session, three to six lines each. If you write an entry later, mark it: *2026-10-03 (retrospective, written 10-05)*.

Each entry answers:

- **Date** (mark retrospective entries)
- **What I tried and expected**
- **What happened**, what was difficult, or what I checked
- **What I did next**
- **What {{AI tool}} or another person contributed**, and whether I accepted, changed or rejected it
- **What I understand now**, and what I still do not understand
- **Evidence** I can point to (a file, a test, an output, a draft, a prompt excerpt) and my next step

How it is graded: five rows, 2 points each, out of 10. A failed attempt can earn full credit. More time, more mistakes or more versions earn nothing extra. If the work was straightforward, say so and name what you checked. The grade scores the record, not a guess about you; if the log and the work do not match, we talk.

AI may help you organize your notes. It must not invent your attempts, struggles, timestamps, understanding or anyone's approval. A fabricated log is treated as more serious than undisclosed AI use.
