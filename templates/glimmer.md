## How it is graded (/20)

Five dimensions, 0 to 4 each: **why** (each key choice justified against alternatives), **usefulness** (someone else could act on it), **mechanism** (the concept used as a tool, not named), **defensibility** (anticipates how it fails, states trade-offs), **specificity** (numbers, mechanisms, timelines, named alternatives). There is no score for being right.
