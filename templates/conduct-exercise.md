# Conduct it: {{module task in a few words}}

**Time:** about {{minutes}} minutes. **Level of AI use:** {{copy editor / research assistant / supercollaborator}}.
**You will need:** {{AI tool available to students}}, {{independent source or tool for checking}}.

## 1. Predict and frame (before any AI)

Write, and date:

- what you expect the answer or result to be, and how confident you are;
- the problem in your own words, including {{the constraint or value that matters in this discipline}};
- what would show your expectation is wrong.

Do not change this after you see the AI's output. A wrong prediction costs nothing; a rewritten one does.

## 2. Your first attempt

{{The part of the task students do before AI, e.g. "Sketch the reduction", "Draft the hypothesis and the controls", "Write the first paragraph of the memo"}}.

## 3. Delegate one bounded task

Ask {{the AI tool}} to {{bounded task}}. Give it {{what to give it}}. Do not give it {{what stays with you}}.
Save the exact prompt and the full output.

## 4. Audit first, then check

Before you look anything up, for each claim in the output that matters, write:

- **Verdict:** plausible, suspicious, or wrong;
- **Target:** what you would check;
- **Mechanism:** why you think so, from what you know about {{the domain}}.

Then check, using {{an independent source: a hand calculation, the primary paper, the dataset, the lab manual, a test}}. A second AI agreeing is not a check. Record which verdicts held.

## 5. What you add

Write a short memo with five labelled parts:

1. **What the AI produced** (one paragraph).
2. **What I add** that it could not: {{domain judgment, context, values}}.
3. **Why it can be trusted:** the checks and what they showed.
4. **Why it is right here:** for this situation, these people, this purpose.
5. **Recommendation**, signed by you.

## 6. Sign-off

- **Checked:** what, and how.
- **Not checked:** what you did not verify. "Nothing" is rarely true.
- **Not claimed:** what this work does not show.
- **Split:** what you did, what the AI did, where you overrode it.
