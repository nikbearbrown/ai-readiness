### What every use of AI must meet

- **You can explain it.** You must be able to explain anything you submit, in your own words, when asked. Not being able to is the problem, not the AI.
- **You disclose it.** Say which tool you used, for what, what you kept, what you rejected and why. {{Where: "in the Frictional log", "in the AI Use Disclosure at the end of each submission"}}.
- **You never invent the record.** AI may help organize your notes. It must not invent your attempts, struggles, results, timestamps, understanding or anyone's approval. A fabricated record is treated more seriously than undisclosed use.
- **You check it independently.** Agreement between two AI tools is not a check. Check against {{the course's independent evidence: a calculation by hand, a primary source, a lab result, a test}}.
- **AI use alone is not misconduct.** Undisclosed use, invented records and work you cannot explain are.
